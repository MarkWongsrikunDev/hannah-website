About

Global program leadership with a focus on delivery.

Hannah Skelly has twelve years of experience working with international donors, private partners, and governments to provide technical support, lead project design, and implement programs in health, education, and ICT4D internationally.

Her work spans multi-million-dollar initiatives from concept through completion, coordinating teams, managing communications with clients, and ensuring the delivery of technically sound, sustainable solutions for a wide range of public and private stakeholders.
